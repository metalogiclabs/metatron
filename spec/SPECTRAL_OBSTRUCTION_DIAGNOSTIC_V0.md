# Spectral Obstruction Diagnostic V0

**Status:** disposable falsification experiment  
**Branch:** `spectral-obstruction-diagnostic-v0`

## Question

Can the least adjacency eigenvalue / clique structure of Metatron's existing
graphs provide a useful derived signal for residuals, capability discoveries,
or quotient collapses without adding authority to the Nucleus?

The experiment is deliberately read-only and non-authoritative.

## Two candidate graph surfaces

1. **Behavioral-equivalence graph.** States are adjacent exactly when they are
   continuation-safe equivalent.
2. **Live warrant graph.** Live warrants are vertices and premise links are
   symmetrized into an undirected diagnostic graph.

The finite fixtures are not invented for the spectral test. They reuse the
three-state Future Observations quotient and the Minimal Warrant Graph V0
lifecycle already present in the repository.

## Promotion gate

Keep a spectral diagnostic only if it supplies information that is not already
forced by the exact quotient and is stable under semantically irrelevant
provenance additions.

The following are falsifiers:

- the equivalence graph is automatically a disjoint union of cliques;
- a successful residual repair can make the negative mode stronger rather than
  weaker;
- adding a diagnostic-only measurement can change the spectrum while leaving
  live capability/relation authority unchanged.

## Result

All three falsifiers occur.

The behavioral-equivalence graph is a cluster graph by construction, so its
clique/spectral structure mostly restates the quotient.

On the raw warrant graph, successful capability and relation additions move the
least eigenvalue farther negative in the retained V0 lifecycle. A
measurement-only provenance node also changes the spectrum without changing the
live capability/relation authority signature.

Therefore raw graph spectrum is **not promoted into the Metatron core**.

A future use remains possible outside authority: large weighted or approximate
search graphs may use spectral modes as a disposable prioritization heuristic,
but any such use must earn its value on held-out prediction before retention.
