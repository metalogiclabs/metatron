# Blind Grammar-Shape Genesis V1 — Pre-Registration

**Frozen before any V1 hidden challenge exists.**

## Objective

Test prospectively whether a fresh hidden consequential residual can force
Metatron to identify the **minimum useful grammar arity and an extensional
operator shape** before reveal.

The challenge is generated inside GitHub Actions only after this
pre-registration is committed.

## Hidden-world protocol

Each run must create a fresh randomized six-bit world.

The generator must:

1. enumerate anonymous local Boolean rewrite candidates of arity 1, 2, and 3;
2. represent every candidate extensionally by:
   - its arity,
   - an ordered local scope,
   - a target-bit-bijective truth table;
3. choose a hidden planted minimum arity from `{2,3}`;
4. choose a hidden planted candidate at that arity;
5. define the **current interface** as the common refinement generated by:
   - the seed parity observation; and
   - every public candidate strictly below the hidden planted arity;
6. define the **target future interface** as the current interface plus the
   planted candidate's induced observation;
7. reject and resample until:
   - the current residual is nonempty;
   - every candidate below the planted arity has exactly zero residual gain;
   - the planted candidate closes the entire consequential residual;
   - at least one exact closer exists at the planted arity;
   - the current interface is not already target-sufficient.

Candidate IDs, state order, scopes, and truth tables must be shuffled or sampled
freshly for each run.

## Public artifact

The predictor receives only:

- anonymous state IDs and their six-bit vectors in randomized order;
- the seed observation kind;
- current interface class IDs;
- target future class IDs;
- anonymous candidate records containing arity, scope, and extensional truth
  table;
- a SHA-256 commitment to the hidden artifact;
- the exact pre-registration commit.

It does **not** receive:

- the planted candidate ID;
- the planted minimum arity;
- the generator seed;
- the canonical hidden answer.

## Consequential residual

The predictor constructs

[
U=
{{x,y}:C(x)=C(y)land T(x)
e T(y)},
]

where (C) is the public current-interface class and (T) is the public
target-future class.

For every candidate (g), it computes its induced seed observation after the
declared local rewrite and therefore its exact gain on (U).

## Frozen prediction rule

Using public data only:

1. compute (U);
2. evaluate every candidate extensionally;
3. group candidates by arity;
4. find the least arity (a^star) with an exact closer of (U);
5. separately record the maximum residual gain at every smaller arity;
6. emit the complete family of exact closers at (a^star);
7. choose a canonical closer by semantic ordering of
   `(arity, scope, truth_table, anonymous_id)`;
8. emit the canonical closer's extensional shape;
9. bind the prediction cryptographically to the public artifact;
10. repeat after a fixed deterministic state relabeling and require invariance
    of minimum arity and exact-closer count.

No target-specific tuning is permitted after challenge generation.

## Reveal gates

R1. Hidden artifact matches its public commitment.

R2. Prediction is bound to the exact public artifact.

R3. Public consequential residual is nonempty.

R4. Every candidate below the hidden planted arity has zero residual gain.

R5. The planted hidden candidate closes the full residual.

R6. Predicted minimum arity equals the true minimum arity.

R7. The canonical predicted closer closes the full residual.

R8. No lower-arity candidate closes the residual.

R9. The planted candidate belongs to the predicted minimum-arity exact-closer
family.

R10. Deterministic state relabeling preserves the minimum arity and the number
of minimum-arity exact closers.

## Verdict

- `BLIND_GRAMMAR_SHAPE_PASS`: R1–R10 all pass.
- `BLIND_GRAMMAR_SHAPE_FAIL`: any reveal gate fails.

## Claim boundary

A positive result establishes blind prospective selection of the minimum useful
arity and an extensional operator shape **inside a declared finite arity-1/2/3
meta-grammar**.

It does not establish unrestricted syntax invention, unbounded arity, or
discovery outside the declared extensional grammar.
