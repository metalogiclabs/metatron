# Nucleus Genesis V0

**Status:** executable experiment  
**Branch:** `nucleus-genesis-v0`

Nucleus Genesis is the smallest Metatron experiment in which the active
representation itself changes only when an exact obstruction proves the current
representation insufficient.

Its developmental law is:

In V0 every term is finite and exhaustive.

## Constitution

1. Residuals earn generators.
2. Warrants earn relations.
3. Free completion exposes compositional consequence.
4. Protected futures determine active identity.
5. Exact obstructions justify lifting.
6. Quotients erase only distinctions no lawful continuation can expose.
7. Promotion, query change, and revocation trigger reclosure.
8. History remains append-only while the live presentation may change.
9. Revocation retracts authority rather than deleting occurrence.
10. If a newly protected future distinguishes an old class, regrow rather than guess.

## Tiny world

The carrier is

[
S={0,1,2}.
]

Genesis starts with only

[
id=(0,1,2)
]

and the protected query `IS_ZERO`. Its exact continuation-safe quotient is

[
{{0},{1,2}}.
]

The target capability

[
step=(1,1,2)
]

is absent from the free compositional closure. Exhaustive closure therefore
issues a residual certificate. That persisted certificate, and only that
certificate, may authorize the one-generator lift introducing `step`.

Finite verification warrants promotion. Reclosure then earns

[
step;step=step.
]

With only `IS_ZERO` protected, states 1 and 2 remain safely merged.

When `IS_ONE` becomes protected, states 1 and 2 receive an exact separating
witness. The machine records `REPRESENTATION_LIFT` and refines to

[
{{0},{1},{2}}.
]

That is the Genesis event: a new protected future proves the old reasoning
space inadequate, so the machine changes that space under warrant rather than
silently changing its ontology.

## Claim boundary

V0 demonstrates this finite developmental shape only. It does not establish a
general Nucleus universal property, universal minimal repair, arbitrary
representation discovery, or open-ended productivity.

The next gate is Lean reference semantics for these exact finite laws, followed
by replacement of the fixture-specific lift with the smallest generic
generator/continuation construction that survives theorem, falsifier, and
ablation.
