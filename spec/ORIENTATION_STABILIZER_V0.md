# Orientation Stabilizer V0

## Objective

Test the smallest exact mathematical claim extracted from the supplied Period
Lattice reflection/reversal prose:

- reflection alone does not preserve the declared orientation-coherence class;
- reversal alone does not preserve it;
- their compound does preserve it;
- within the four transformations generated by the two involutions, the
  compound is the unique non-identity coherence-preserving transformation.

This is a bounded symmetry theorem. It is not a physical phase-transition claim.

## Source-derived content

The source prose supplies the following qualitative structure:

- two operations called reflection and reversal;
- either operation alone does not recover the readable/oriented arrangement;
- applying both together does;
- orientation matters.

The source does not provide a formal state space, action law, energy, topology,
propagation rule, or physical phase-slip dynamics.

## Minimal formalization

We encode orientation by two independent Boolean bits:

1. glyph reflection state;
2. reading-order reversal state.

A state is coherent exactly when the two bits agree. The generated transformation
group has four elements:

- identity: toggle neither bit;
- reflection: toggle only glyph orientation;
- reversal: toggle only reading order;
- compound: toggle both.

This is the smallest faithful finite model of the source's joint-operation claim.

## Census

scripts/orientation_stabilizer_v0.py exhaustively checks all 4 states and all
4 generated transformations.

Expected exact result:

    coherent states = {(0,0), (1,1)}
    stabilizer = {identity, compound}

The two involutions commute in this model, and the compound is their product.

## Formal theorem

formal/Metatron/OrientationStabilizer.lean proves:

- reflection_does_not_preserve;
- reversal_does_not_preserve;
- compound_preserves;
- reflection_reversal_commute;
- stabilizer_exact;
- only_nontrivial_preserver_is_compound.

## Verification boundary

If qualification passes, the warranted statement is only:

> In this declared two-bit orientation model, the exact stabilizer of coherence
> inside the four generated transformations is {identity, compound}.

This does not establish:

- that the Period Lattice itself has exactly this state space;
- that the word-level glyph transformation uniquely determines lattice physics;
- a phase slip/drop in the physical sense;
- propagation, reorientation waves, retroactive interpretation, causality, or
  any natural-world dynamics;
- uniqueness among transformations outside the declared four-element group.
